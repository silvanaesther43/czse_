About Me

Open to collaborations and research opportunities

I am a geographer (UMSA) and I transform maps into decision-making tools, from flood risk models in the Bolivian Andes to open geospatial platforms. I serve as a regional ambassador and am a former president of the YouthMappers chapter. I have led community mapping projects and was selected as a leadership fellow in 2024.

Lately, I have been involved in software development with the IT team at The Consulting Academy. I am also applying my geospatial expertise to broader technological solutions. My main focus is on risk management, spatial analysis, and building resilient, data-driven tools for vulnerable communities.
