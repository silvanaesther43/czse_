About Me

I'm a final year Geographic Engineering student at the Universidad Mayor de San Andrés, collaborating with the Social Technologies Laboratory. Since 2022, I've been involved with YouthMappers, chairing the UMSA Bolivia chapter, and supporting projects mapping transportation routes, nature protection, socio-environmental risks, and sustainable tourism. Along with my chapter, I participated in the YouthMappers LATAM 2023 microgrants. In 2024, I had the valuable opportunity to participate as a YouthMappers Leadership Fellow. As an active member of the Latin American Journal of Geography Students RELEG and working as a teaching assistant, my goal is to expand the reach of geographic information, helping young people harness the potential of cartography and geospatial analysis. Passionate about statistics, I'm also interested in solving problems through simulation models and have a special interest in risk management. With experience in Geographic Information Systems, remote sensing, and geospatial technologies, I seek to explore innovative solutions and work alongside communities to achieve effective territorial management, supporting humanitarian aid initiatives and seeking alternatives to build a more resilient world.
